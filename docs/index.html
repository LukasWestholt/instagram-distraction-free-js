iOS Safari: a few extra steps
  1. Install Tampermonkey or Userscripts from the App Store.
  2. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions, tap your extension, and switch it on.
  3. Under “Allow Extension to Read and Modify Webpages”, set instagram.com (or All Websites) to Allow.
  4. Tap the install link above from Safari. With Userscripts, long‑press it, choose Download Linked File, and save it to your configured sync folder instead.

03

Open Instagram

Visit instagram.com — the script activates on its own. Find the “IG Clean” button (bottom‑left on desktop, bottom‑right on mobile/iOS) to change what gets filtered.

What changes

  • Sponsored posts are stripped from the feed data before your browser renders them — not hidden with CSS after the fact.
  • Suggested content — “suggested for you” posts and profile cards — is gone.
  • Read receipts are blocked; the “Seen” timestamp never gets sent.
  • Autoplay video loads muted, by default.
  • Notification permission dialogs are suppressed before they fire.
  • Everything else stays — the accounts you follow, and the ability to turn any of this back off from the settings panel.
